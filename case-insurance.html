Reflection

Lesson Learned

The team shipped 500+ pages in phase one in November 2022. Looking back, I learned to use design system components to move faster, communicate design decisions clearly across stakeholders, and work well within a multidisciplinary team.

Designing for both mobile and web

After spotting a gap in mobile responsiveness, I wrote style guidelines covering both desktop and mobile views. This sped up QA and cut review time.

Working with developers

I partnered with developers on clear specs and a padding guide. This streamlined communication and cut down on quality issues.

Broader team-wide enabling

Next time, I'd give developers direct Figma access instead of exported, manually labeled images. This would mean fewer requirement checks and less back-and-forth.
