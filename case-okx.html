Simplify the DeFi earning experience

How redesigning OKX On-chain Earn's subscription journey led to 52% better conversion rates by prioritizing familiarity and improved information hierarchy.

The challenge

The Problem

The subscription experience felt overwhelming and hard to understand

Users faced 40+ products with no clear structure or starting point. Each product had its own reward and redemption rules. This complexity reduced subscription conversion and drove customer complaints.

Project Goals

We took a phased approach to improve the overall experience.

Phase one

Improve product discovery

Optimizing the landing page to help users quickly find products that interest them.

Phase two

Enhance information clarity during subscription

Making product information more digestible and ensuring critical details are presented more effectively.

Process

I conducted data analysis and an alignment workshop to visualise what pain points existed and when.

Process

User Insights

  1. Users are keen to explore, but need stronger guidance

    Data showed many users explored the landing page independently, moving between pages repeatedly before dropping off. They needed clearer structure and stronger cues on where to start.

  2. Familiar tokens convert faster than high rewards

    We observed a higher and faster subscription rate when users entered from pages showing assets they already held. Existing holdings influenced decisions more than headline APR.

  3. Key product rules are invisible to the user

    Even though the information existed, users struggled to acknowledge critical details like earning schedule and redemption period. These rules were buried in dense paragraphs instead of being surfaced and prioritised.

How might we make DeFi earning feel simple by guiding exploration, leading with familiar assets, and making key rules impossible to miss?

Process

Design Principles

Design for actionability

We explored multiple ways to restructure 40+ products (e.g. Staking/DeFi, Popular, New). These labels added complexity and felt like forced education. We eventually shifted from “teach DeFi categories” to “help users act with familiar assets.”

Design for focus

Most complaints weren’t caused by missing information — they were caused by poor visibility. Users missed key rules because they were buried in dense text.

We tested different placements and formats, and aligned with stakeholders on what must appear before a user commits versus what can appear after.

Solutions

Lead with familiarity to make the catalog actionable

Users didn’t need more categories. They needed a fast way to start from what they already knew. We prioritized tokens users already owned over high APR as the primary entry point.

Surface key rules at the moment of decision

We removed the forced education before subscription that users frequently skipped, and focused on strategically placing information based on decision priority. This makes critical rules impossible to miss during subscription.

Reduce cognitive load with clear hierarchy and plain language

We improved visual hierarchy and made support content more scannable. DeFi jargon was also replaced with easy-to-understand terms with the help of content designers.

Protocol Source of earning
Interest accrual time Reward distribution
Product mechanism When will I start earning?
Outcome

Project Impact

The landing page optimization in Phase One combined with the information clarity improvements in Phase Two enhanced overall user engagement and subscription completion rates.

114% Increase in landing conversion
45% Increase in subscription conversion
52.28% Increase in overall conversion
188% Decrease in time to convert
Outcome

Key Takeaways

  1. Revisit data when unsure about design decisions

  2. Stakeholder alignment prior to design review saves time

  3. Revamp takes time, so be patient and trust the process
